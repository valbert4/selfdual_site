T15 - Fiber/Coset Multiset Match

Status: saturates

Summary

This test asks whether the fiber and coset multisets of a length-40 child can be matched to the forced split rows of the residual. The aggregate matching is feasible for every row that reaches it.

What It Tests

  • Object: the multiset of projection fibers and the multiset of residual split rows.
  • Input: the row (k,a,b), the fiber sizes, and the forced split-row multiset.
  • Necessary condition: a multiset matching (transportation-style) between fibers and split rows exists.
  • Output: rows with no valid matching (none, here).

Result

  • Kills: 0.
  • Status: saturates. The aggregate matching cancels the unknown individual enumerators and is feasible for all surviving rows.

Verification

  • Certificate type: exact (totally-unimodular) transportation feasibility.
  • Reproduce: download the reproduction bundle below and run it.
