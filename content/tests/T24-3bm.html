T24 - Three-Block Mode-1 Per-Coset Tiling

Status: saturates

Summary

This test composes the two filters that actually cut — the three-block split (T05) and per-coset Mode-1 tiling (T18) — into one 16|16|40 per-coset tiling. The composition is feasible for every surviving row.

What It Tests

  • Object: the 16|16|40 three-block split with per-coset tiling of the length-40 block.

  • Necessary condition: a per-coset tiling of the three-block split exists.
  • Output: rows with no valid three-block tiling (none, here).
